# Travel Policy (Demo)

- Trains are preferred for trips under 6 hours; first class is allowed for trips over 2 hours.
- Flights require approval by the team lead and must be economy class.
- Hotels: up to CHF 220 per night in Switzerland, CHF 180 abroad.
- Meals are reimbursed up to CHF 70 per day with receipts.
- Expenses must be submitted within 30 days after the trip.
